Homelette

This is the introduction of my recipe of Homelete.

Ingredients

  • 4 Huevos
  • Queso blanco o quesillo
  • Tortillaz de arina o maiz
  • Jitomate

Instructions

  1. Freir el huevito
  2. Cortar jitomate
  3. Colocar el huevo enzima de la tortilla
  4. Cortar el jitomate y agregarle sal
